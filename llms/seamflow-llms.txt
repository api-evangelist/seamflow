# Seamflow

> Seamflow builds AI-native software for the Testing, Inspection, and Certification (TIC) industry, automating end-to-end regulatory certification workflows for notified bodies and certification organizations while keeping human oversight and accreditation compliance intact.

Seamflow's platform covers four core products: the Notified Body Suite (intake-to-certificate workflow management), Scheduling Agents (automated auditor assignment and travel optimization), Tech File Review (structured technical-documentation assessment against regulatory requirements), and Report Writing (AI-assisted drafting of compliance reports). It is an early-stage enterprise-SaaS company ($4.5M seed) backed by Initialized Capital and Northzone. As of this writing Seamflow publishes no public API, developer portal, SDKs, or OpenAPI specification; this entry indexes its public company surface.
